Modes of warfare co-occur depending on belligerent capabilities and the degree of escalation. Almost every time, the initial phase includes abstract warfare.

# Physical warfare
In this mode, belligerents are actively trying to inflict physical damage to habitat/infrastructure, livestock and humans.

This is the most commonly acknowledged type of warfare.

# Abstract warfare
Abstract warfare operates indirectly through various modes such as Information, economics, psychology/cognitive, and cyber. Unlike physical warfare, these attacks are relatively difficult to identify and confirm due to their complex and abstract nature.

Abstract modes do not operate in isolation but overlap in practical implementation.

These methods and modes are not inherently destructive. Many of these can be useful for sustainability.

## Psychological/cognitive warfare
This includes exploiting human biases.

Identity wiring, herd behaviour, group-think, and biological competition.

belief pre-training, social reality mgmt.

exploiting the belief system.

reward system, error

Use of existing social networks and hierarchies.

## Information warfare
This is the most fundamental and encompassing mode.

Maintaining knowledge systems, information gathering

Information design for psychological warfare

## Economic warfare
This includes exploiting economic networks/entities.

Imposing taxes, blockades, and boycotting targeted identity,

Use of existing economic high ground to control market access. Controlling and disrupting supply chains.

Economic networks directly integrate with the reward system, structure-agency on the individual level.

## Cyber warfare
Using digital infrastructure as media to:

Gather information.

Identify behavioural patterns.

Propagate disinformation.

Conduct economic and psychological operations.

# List of exploits

### False feedback
Inducing false rewards and penalties to target and inflict mental injury and disorient the target.

### Controlling action space
Exploiting free will by controlling action space, limiting choices.

### Information ingestion
- Forcing conversation through close social nodes such as friends, workplace colleagues, etc., using digital social media.


- Goals
  - Forming beliefs, opinions, reinforce biases to exploit and sustain operations.

### Identity fishing
Propagating intricately designed information through various media targeted towards specific identities. Although information accessible publicly as neutral, its perception by targeted identities is driven by high salience towards self two-traits and pre-trained shared beliefs. Effects include reinforcing/altering identity wiring, distorting perceived identity norm.
